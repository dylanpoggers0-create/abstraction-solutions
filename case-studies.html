AI CHATBOT · EDUCATION

CBHS Information Chatbot

The challenge

Students and faculty at Christian Brothers High School needed fast, accurate answers to questions about the school, and a normal chatbot would just guess.

What we built

A chatbot that uses RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): before it answers, it finds the right facts in the school's own information, so its answers come from real sources.

MACHINE LEARNING · RETAIL
