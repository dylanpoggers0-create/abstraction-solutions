SITE ASSISTANT

An AI site assistant that runs in the browser.

This assistant answers questions from our public pages and shows the passage it used. It runs in the visitor's browser, so questions do not go to our servers. That is the simplest on-device demo we can show you — and we can build a similar assistant for your site.

How it works

  1. We read your public pages and split them into short passages by heading.
  2. Those passages are turned into a small search index that ships with your site.
  3. When a visitor asks a question, their browser finds the best passage and shows it with a link to the page it came from. If nothing matches well enough, it says so instead of guessing.
  4. The index rebuilds itself whenever a page changes, so answers stay current.

Why run it in the browser

There is no server to pay for or keep awake, answers come back right away, and visitors' questions stay on their own device.
