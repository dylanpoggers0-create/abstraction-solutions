What we do for clubs and nonprofits

Written for a club president, secretary, or nonprofit director. No technical background needed.

  • Club and nonprofit websites

    A site members can use: who you are, when you meet, what your programs are, and how to reach an officer. Built for a Rotary club, a civic club, or another nonprofit.

  • Member and volunteer communication

    One place for announcements, so the same update is not copied across email threads. Officers point people to the page instead of retyping it.

  • Event RSVPs and signups

    A form for a meeting, a fundraiser, or a volunteer shift. Responses go by email to the officer you name. You keep the guest list.

  • Newsletters

    A signup on the site, and a straightforward way to send news to members who asked for it. People can unsubscribe on their own.

  • Dues and donation flows

    A page that explains what dues cover, where a gift goes, and the link or instructions your treasurer already uses. The club stays in charge of the money. We do not publish a price list.

  • Simple AI automations and AI education

    Small helpers, such as sorting incoming messages or drafting a reminder you still approve before it goes out. Plus a plain-language intro so members can learn what AI is. See AI education.
