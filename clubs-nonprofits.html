What we do for nonprofits

Written for a director or volunteer lead. No technical background needed.

  • Nonprofit websites

    A site people can use: who you are, what you do, when things happen, and how to reach someone.

  • Volunteer communication

    One place for announcements, so the same update is not copied across email threads.

  • Event RSVPs and signups

    A form for a meeting, a fundraiser, or a volunteer shift. Responses go by email to the person you name.

  • Newsletters

    A signup on the site, and a straightforward way to send news to people who asked for it.

  • Donation pages

    A page that explains where a gift goes and uses the payment link or instructions you already have. If you also charge dues, the page can say that too. You stay in charge of the money. We do not publish a price list.

  • Simple AI and AI education

    Small helpers, such as sorting incoming messages or drafting a reminder you still approve. Plus a plain-language intro to AI. See AI education.
