Data Processing Addendum

Effective November 18, 2026

The short version: when we build something that handles your customers' or members' personal data (a chatbot, an automation, a website with a form), you stay in control of that data. We only use it to do the work you asked for, keep it confidential and secure, tell you quickly if something goes wrong, and delete or return it when the project ends. The providers we rely on are listed below.

When this applies

This addendum is part of our Terms of Service and your project's service agreement. It applies whenever we process personal data on your behalf while building, testing or supporting your project. For that data, you are the controller (you decide why and how it's used) and we are the processor (we handle it for you). Our own customer records, like your order and contact details, are covered by our Privacy Policy instead.

What we promise

  • Your instructions only: we use your data only to deliver and support your project, as set out in your service agreement and your written instructions. We'll tell you if we think an instruction breaks the law.
  • No selling, no training: we never sell your data, and we never use it to train AI models or for our own purposes.
  • Least data: we ask for, copy and keep only what the work needs. Where we can, we use test or sample data instead of real records.
  • Confidentiality: only people working on your project can access your data, and they're bound to keep it confidential.
  • Security: we use encryption in transit (HTTPS), password-protected admin tools that support two-factor sign-in, private databases, and we don't put your data in public storage or public code repositories.
  • Subprocessors: we only use the providers listed below. We'll update this page and, for active projects, email you before adding a new provider that will receive your data, so you can object.
  • Breaches: if we learn your data was accessed without permission, we'll email you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, with what we know and what we're doing about it.
  • Helping you: we'll help you answer requests from people to see, correct, export or delete their data, and give you the information you reasonably need to show you're meeting your own obligations.
  • When the project ends: we delete or hand back your data within 30 days, unless you ask us to keep supporting the project or the law requires us to keep it.

AI in your project

If your project uses an AI service (for example Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic or an open-source model), we'll tell you which one before it handles your data, configure it so your data isn't used to train the provider's models where the provider offers that option, and add a clear notice for your users that they're talking to or being helped by AI. Chatbots we build show crisis resources if a user says they may hurt themselves.
