Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Updated November 18, 2026

The short version: we only collect what we need to quote, build and get paid for your project. We never sell your information. Card details go straight to Stripe, never to us. Some tools on this site use AI, and we say exactly which ones below. You can cancel a project, delete your account or unsubscribe yourself, anytime, and email us to see or delete your data.

Who we are

Abstraction Solutions ("we", "us") is an AI education and software business based in Tennessee, USA. This policy explains what information abstraction-solutions.com collects and what we do with it. Questions? Email dylanpogue@abstraction-solutions.com.

What we collect

  • Quote requests: the project description you type into the free quote tool.
  • Site assistant: nothing. The chat searches our pages inside your own browser, so your questions never leave your device. To search smarter, your browser may download a small public search model from Hugging Face and jsDelivr, like loading any other file.
  • Orders: your name, email address, the service you pick and your project details. We also store your confirmation number, order status, quote and whether it has been paid.
  • Accounts (optional): if you sign in with Google or with an emailed sign-in link, we keep your email address, your name, your Google account ID (if you used Google) and when you last signed in, so you can see your orders in one place. Your browser keeps you signed in on your device; we don't use tracking cookies. You can delete your account yourself anytime from the order page.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number never touches our servers. We only receive a record that the payment succeeded.
  • Mailing list: if you sign up on our homepage, your first and last name, email address, and the date you agreed, so we can send special offers, early access to videos and products, newsletters, giveaways, announcements and updates. Joining is free and has no commitment. You can unsubscribe at any time: every email has an unsubscribe link, or email us and we'll remove you.
  • Emails you send us: whatever you choose to include.
  • Certification Finder: the field you want and the résumé text you paste or upload are used once to make suggestions and are not saved. A résumé PDF you upload is read in our server's memory to pull out its text, then thrown away: it is never written to disk, never put in file storage, and never logged. If you vote on a suggestion, we keep the vote with an anonymous, coarse summary (your field, experience level and a few background signals), never your résumé.
  • Technical data: to stop spam and abuse, our server records your IP address and the time of each request for up to 2 days (rate limiting). For the Certification Finder, résumé upload, sign-in and order forms, it also keeps a coded summary of each request (how often and how fast requests arrive, their size and what kind of browser sent them) with a scrambled, one-way version of your IP address for up to 30 days, to spot automated abuse. It never blocks anyone on its own.
  • Service agreement signatures: when you sign your project's service agreement on the order page, we keep a signature record: the name you typed, your email, the time, your IP address, your browser type, and an exact copy of the agreement you signed with its digital fingerprint. It proves what was agreed, and we keep it for 7 years after the project ends.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Visit counts: we use GoatCounter to count page visits and how many quotes and orders are started. It uses no cookies and doesn't collect personal information, so we can see totals but not who you are.

How we use AI

We're an AI company, so we want to be clear about where AI touches your information:

  • Instant quote: the project description you type is sent to Google Gemini, which estimates the size of the project. The estimate is automatic and not a final price. A person reviews every final quote before you're asked to pay anything.
  • Certification Finder: your field and résumé text are sent to Google Gemini to pick and explain certifications. If Gemini is busy, our own in-house model, which runs on our server, makes the picks instead. It also learns from the anonymous votes described above, never from résumés.
  • Abuse protection: our in-house model looks at the coded request summaries described above to spot automated abuse. It flags requests for a person to review and never blocks anyone on its own.
  • Site assistant: it searches our own pages in your browser. It doesn't write answers with AI and doesn't send your questions anywhere.
  • Building your project: we use AI tools to help write code and content. A person designs, reviews and tests everything before it's delivered to you.

AI never makes final decisions about your quote, order, refund or payments; a person does. We don't use your personal information to train AI models. Google handles the text we send to Gemini under its Gemini API terms, and on free-tier use Google may use that text to improve its own products, which is one more reason to leave personal details out. Please don't type passwords or sensitive personal information into the quote tool or Certification Finder.

If you're going through something hard: if you write in any of our forms that you might hurt yourself, we show you crisis resources (like the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) instead of an AI answer, and we don't send your message to the AI. We also email ourselves a short alert, without your message, so a person can check in if you left your email.

How we use it

  • To generate your instant estimate and prepare your final quote.
  • To send you your confirmation, quote, payment receipt and project updates by email.
  • If you joined our mailing list, to send you offers, newsletters and announcements until you unsubscribe.
  • To build and support the project you ordered.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

Services we use

We rely on a few trusted providers to run the site. Each only gets the information it needs. The full list, with locations, is on our subprocessors page.

  • Google Sign-In: lets you sign in with your Google account if you choose to. Google tells us your email, name and account ID; we never see your Google password.
  • Google Workspace: runs our business email inbox, so emails you send us are stored there.
  • Google Gemini: reads the project description you type into the quote tool to estimate its size, and the résumé text and field you give the Certification Finder to suggest certifications. Please don't include passwords or sensitive personal information there.
  • Neon: the private database where orders, accounts, signature records and mailing-list signups are stored. It is not publicly accessible.
  • Render and GitHub Pages: host our server and website.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • GoatCounter: counts visits without cookies or personal data.
  • Hugging Face and jsDelivr: serve the public search model and code files your browser downloads for the site assistant. They see your IP address like any website you load files from.
  • Stripe: processes payments (see Stripe's privacy policy).

Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or tracking cookies, so there's no cookie banner to click through. GoatCounter counts visits without cookies. Your browser only stores what the site needs to work: your sign-in (if you sign in), a copy of your order list so it opens instantly (removed when you sign out), and drafts so a form isn't lost if you refresh. Stripe's payment form loads only when you choose to pay, and Google's sign-in button loads only on the order page, where you choose whether to use it.

Files and storage

We don't keep uploaded files. The only upload on our site is the résumé PDF for the Certification Finder, and it's discarded right after its text is read. We don't use public file storage or public storage buckets. Your information lives only in our private database and email inbox. Our website's code is public on GitHub, and it contains no customer information.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to deliver and support your project and to keep accurate business and tax records. Signed service agreements and their signature records are kept for 7 years after the project ends. After that, we delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner, and we will unless we're required to keep it.

Your choices

  • Cancel a project: look up your order on the order page and press "Cancel this project". Nothing more is due after that.
  • Download your data: on the order page, signed-in customers can press "Download my data", and anyone can download everything we hold for an order with its email and confirmation number.
  • Delete your account: sign in on the order page and press "Delete account". Your orders stay on record for business and tax purposes, but they're no longer linked to an account.
  • Unsubscribe: one click from the link at the bottom of any of our emails.

You can also ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing dylanpogue@abstraction-solutions.com from the address you ordered with. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Our site and server use HTTPS encryption, the admin area is password protected, and payments are handled by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.
