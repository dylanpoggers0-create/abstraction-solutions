Privacy Policy

Effective November 4, 2026

The short version: we only collect what we need to quote, build and get paid for your project. We never sell your information. Card details go straight to Stripe, never to us. Email us any time to see or delete your data.

Who we are

Abstraction Solutions ("we", "us") is an AI education and software business based in Tennessee, USA. This policy explains what information abstraction-solutions.com collects and what we do with it. Questions? Email dylanpogue@abstraction-solutions.com.

What we collect

  • Quote requests: the project description you type into the free quote tool.
  • Demo assistant: the questions you type into the chat. They aren't saved to our database; your IP address is kept briefly in server memory for rate limiting.
  • Orders: your name, email address, the service you pick and your project details. We also store your confirmation number, order status, quote and whether it has been paid.
  • Payments: handled entirely by Stripe. Your card number never touches our servers. We only receive a record that the payment succeeded.
  • Newsletter: if you subscribe, your email address, so we can send our free progress update every 2 weeks. Every newsletter has an unsubscribe link, or email us and we'll remove you.
  • Emails you send us: whatever you choose to include.
  • Technical data: your IP address is kept briefly in server memory to stop spam and abuse (rate limiting). It is not saved to our database.

We don't use advertising trackers, and we don't sell or rent your information to anyone.

Visit counts: we use GoatCounter to count page visits and how many quotes and orders are started. It uses no cookies and doesn't collect personal information, so we can see totals but not who you are.

How we use it

  • To generate your instant estimate and prepare your final quote.
  • To send you your confirmation, quote, payment receipt and project updates by email.
  • To build and support the project you ordered.
  • To keep the site secure and prevent abuse.

Services we use

We rely on a few trusted providers to run the site. Each only gets the information it needs:

  • Google Gemini: reads the project description you type into the quote tool to estimate its size, and the questions you ask the demo assistant so it can answer them. Please don't include passwords or sensitive personal information in either.
  • Neon: the database where orders are stored.
  • Render and GitHub Pages: host our server and website.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • GoatCounter: counts visits without cookies or personal data.
  • Buttondown: stores newsletter subscribers' email addresses and sends the newsletter.
  • Stripe: processes payments (see Stripe's privacy policy).

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to deliver and support your project and to keep accurate business and tax records. After that, we delete them. You can ask us to delete your data sooner, and we will unless we're required to keep it.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing dylanpogue@abstraction-solutions.com from the address you ordered with. We'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Our site and server use HTTPS encryption, the admin area is password protected, and payments are handled by Stripe. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Our services are meant for businesses and adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
