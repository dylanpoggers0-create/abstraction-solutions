AUTOMATION RECIPE · CPA AND ACCOUNTING FIRMS

Client document checklist

Each client gets a personal checklist of the documents you need, and reminders for anything still missing.

The problem today

Every season starts with chasing documents. Clients aren't sure what to send, and your team tracks who's missing what by hand.

How the automation works

  1. Build the checklist

    Each client gets a checklist based on last year's return type and your intake questions.

  2. Send it

    The checklist goes out with a link to the secure upload portal you already use.

  3. Track

    As uploads come in, items are checked off.

  4. Remind

    Clients get polite reminders for whatever is still missing.

  5. See the status

    Your team gets one list of which clients are complete and which aren't.

You stay in control
  • Tax advice always comes from your team, never the AI.
  • Client documents stay in the systems you already use.
  • You approve every reminder template.
