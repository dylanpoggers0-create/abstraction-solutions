AUTOMATION RECIPE · DENTAL OFFICES

After-hours requests and recall reminders

Patient messages that arrive after closing get sorted and answered first thing, and patients due for a cleaning get a friendly reminder.

The problem today

Patients email or fill out your website form after hours, and those requests wait until someone has time the next day. Patients who are due for a cleaning slip through the cracks unless someone works the list.

How the automation works

  1. Collect

    After-hours messages from your website form and email land in one place.

  2. Sort

    AI tags each message: new patient, reschedule, billing question or possibly urgent. Possibly urgent ones go to the top for your team.

  3. Draft

    It writes a reply to each one in your office's voice, using only information you've approved, like hours, location and insurance you accept.

  4. Review

    Your front desk approves or edits the replies in the morning. Nothing goes out without approval unless you choose otherwise.

  5. Remind

    From a list of patients due for a cleaning (an export from your scheduling software), it sends reminder emails with a link to book.

You stay in control
  • Clinical and urgent questions always go to a person.
  • The AI never gives medical advice.
  • You decide which replies can send on their own.
