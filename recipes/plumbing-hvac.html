AUTOMATION RECIPE · PLUMBING AND HVAC

Quote intake and emergency triage

Every request becomes a complete job ticket, and emergencies are flagged the moment they come in.

The problem today

Requests arrive by email and web form with missing details, so your team spends time going back and forth before anyone can quote or schedule. Real emergencies can get buried in routine requests.

How the automation works

  1. Ask the right questions

    A short online form or email reply collects what your techs need: the problem, the area, photos and how soon.

  2. Triage

    AI flags likely emergencies by your rules (for example no heat or an active leak) and sorts the rest by job type.

  3. Safety first

    For dangerous situations you define, like a gas smell, the customer immediately gets the safety instructions you approve.

  4. Job ticket

    Each request becomes a clean summary your tech can quote from, with the photos attached.

  5. Reply

    The customer gets a confirmation and next steps in your words, and you get suggested time slots from your calendar to confirm.

You stay in control
  • You set what counts as an emergency.
  • You approve every safety message.
  • Prices and appointments are always confirmed by your team.
