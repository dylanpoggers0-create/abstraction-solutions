Maintenance request triage

Tenant requests get categorized, prioritized and routed to the right vendor with a ready-to-use work order.

The problem today

Maintenance requests come in by email, text and portal with different levels of detail. Someone has to read each one, decide how urgent it is, find the right vendor and keep the tenant updated.

How the automation works

  1. Collect

    Tenants submit requests with photos through a form or email.

  2. Categorize

    AI labels each request (plumbing, electrical, appliance, pests and so on) and sets urgency by your rules.

  3. Route

    It drafts a work order for the right vendor from your contact list, ready for you to send.

  4. Update tenants

    Tenants get status messages you've approved, so they're not left wondering.

  5. Summarize

    You get a weekly summary of open requests by property.

You stay in control
  • You approve which vendor gets each job.
  • Emergency rules are yours to set.
  • Tenant messages use wording you approve.
