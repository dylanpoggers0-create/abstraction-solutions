Rebooking nudges

Clients get a friendly nudge to rebook when they're due for their next visit.

The problem today

Regulars forget to rebook, and reaching out to each one by hand takes time nobody has between appointments.

How the automation works

  1. Learn the rhythm

    From your appointment history, it works out each client's usual gap between visits.

  2. Nudge

    When a client is due, they get a short, friendly message with your booking link.

  3. Skip the booked

    Clients who already have an appointment don't get a nudge.

  4. Respect opt-outs

    Anyone who opts out stops getting messages.

  5. Win-back list

    Each month you get a list of clients who haven't come back, for a personal note.

You stay in control
  • You write or approve the message style.
  • You choose how often anyone can be contacted.
  • Opt-outs are honored automatically.
