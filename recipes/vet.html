AUTOMATION RECIPE · VETERINARY CLINICS

Vaccine reminders

Pet owners get a personal reminder when a vaccine is coming due, with a link to book.

The problem today

Keeping up with vaccine due dates means someone pulling lists and sending reminders by hand, and it's easy to fall behind on busy weeks.

How the automation works

  1. Export

    Start from a list of patients and vaccine due dates exported from your practice software.

  2. Find who's due

    It picks out pets with vaccines coming due soon.

  3. Remind

    Owners get a friendly email with the pet's name, the vaccine and a link to book.

  4. Follow up

    If the list shows no appointment yet, a second reminder goes out later. It skips anyone already booked.

  5. Summarize

    Your team sees who was reminded and who still needs a personal touch.

You stay in control
  • Medical questions always go to your staff.
  • You approve the reminder wording.
  • Owners can opt out at any time.
