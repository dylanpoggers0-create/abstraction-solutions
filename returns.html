Returns & Refunds

Effective November 4, 2026 · Updated November 9, 2026

The short version: you pay one stage at a time, so you're never far ahead of the work. Cancel anytime and nothing more is due. A stage you've paid for that we haven't started comes back to you in full. After delivery, every project comes with one month of free bug fixes.

Refunds

Projects are paid in stages (Planning, Coding, Testing, Done), each when it begins. If you cancel, any payment that's waiting is withdrawn and nothing more is due. For stages you've already paid:

  • Paid, but we haven't started it: full refund of that stage.
  • In progress: partial refund of that stage, based on the work completed in it when you cancel. We'll explain how we worked it out.
  • Finished stages: not refundable, because that work has been done.
  • After delivery: no refund. Instead, you're covered by the free bug-fix period below.

Free bug fixes for one month

For 30 days after we deploy your project, we'll fix bugs and errors in the work we delivered at no cost.

This covers problems with our work. It doesn't cover new features, changes to the original requirements, or problems caused by changes made by someone else or by third-party services. We're happy to quote those separately.

How to request a refund or a fix

  • Email dylanpogue@abstraction-solutions.com from the address you ordered with.
  • Include your 8-digit confirmation number and what you need.
  • We'll reply within 2 business days.

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Depending on your bank, they usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.
